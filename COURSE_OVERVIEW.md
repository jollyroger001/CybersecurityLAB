# Cybersecurity Specialist Training Labs
This repository documents my technical activities during the 240h professional specialization course.

## Key Modules Covered:
* **Exploitation**: Buffer Overflow, Metasploit, and Ransomware simulations.
* **Network Security**: MITM attacks, ARP spoofing, and DoS simulation.
* **Analysis**: Malware analysis and cryptographic decryption (RSA).
* **OSINT**: Advanced information gathering and vulnerability research.
